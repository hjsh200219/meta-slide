Needle Point Records · Staff Handbook1

We pay wages for one thing:
a customer leaving with a record they will play a second time.

Not the best-seller, and not the busiest shop. We call it the “second-play test”: when this customer comes back next time and says “I listened to that one again”, you did your job this week.

So we do not push stock. However much we have on the shelf, it is never traded for a customer's ears.

What we reward, and what we do not

WE DO NOT PAY FOR

The highest sales total of the week.

Moving the oldest stock first.

Talking over a customer who is listening.

WE DO PAY FOR

A customer who says they played it twice.

An honest “this one is not for you”.

A name and a taste you remember next visit.
