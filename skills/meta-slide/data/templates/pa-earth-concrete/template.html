Principles· 01

Three rules of the
studio.

Rule 01
Show the structure
Beams, slabs and joints stay visible. Nothing decorative hides how the building stands.
Rule 02
Let daylight work
Openings are sized to the sun path first and to the facade grid second.
Rule 03
Build to last
Specify materials that age well and need little maintenance for fifty years.
