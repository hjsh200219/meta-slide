Why it works

Four reasons the edge wins.

01 · LATENCY
Nearest node serves
Requests resolve within one network hop of the user, not across a continent.
02 · COST
Less backbone traffic
Cached embeddings and quantized weights stay local, cutting egress sharply.
03 · RESILIENCE
No single region to lose
Traffic reroutes between nodes automatically when one degrades.
04 · PRIVACY
Data stays in region
Inputs are processed where they originate and are not shipped to a core cluster.

Request path

From prompt to answer in four steps.

STEP 01
Route
Anycast sends the call to the closest healthy node.
STEP 02
Load
The warm model shard is mapped into GPU memory.
STEP 03
Infer
Batched decoding runs with speculative sampling.
STEP 04
Stream
Tokens stream back while the cache is refreshed.
