ii. What deliberation changes

Three things the model does differently

Note 01

It drafts first

Candidate answers are produced and compared internally before any is committed to the reader.

Note 02

It checks itself

Claims are traced back to their premises, and weak links are flagged instead of smoothed over.

Note 03

It declines well

When the evidence is thin, the model says so plainly rather than inventing a confident answer.
