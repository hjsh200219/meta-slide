Market Renewal · Method

A good market grows from the center out

Most renewals start at the outer ring: new stalls, matching signs. We work backward. Ask why the neighborhood needs a market, then decide how it runs, and only then what it looks like.
Case comparison
The East Road market was renewed from the outside in and had 31% vacancy two years later. The Locust Lane market started from the center, and vacancy is 4%.
