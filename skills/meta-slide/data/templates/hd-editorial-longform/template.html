BLUEWATER OCEAN FOUNDATION · ANNUAL LETTER 2025TO DONORS AND PARTNERS

We turned “protection” from a verb into a ledger anyone can check

Chair of the Board · January 2026

Three years ago, in our first interviews at the fishing harbor, an old captain asked one question: the sea you protect, can I go and see it? That question became the standard for all our work.

In 2025 we stopped reporting “area covered”. Every protected patch of sea now has a public monitoring record: who measured what and when, where the data lives, and when it will be measured again.

The result surprised us. Donors ask finer questions, and partner villages share their own data more readily, because they know the numbers come back to them.

BLUEWATER OCEAN FOUNDATION · ANNUAL LETTER 2025PARTNERS

What changed for the people who live by the sea

BEFORE

Surveys left the village and never came back. Fishers saw reports only when a donor asked for them.

Trust depended on who had visited last.

NOW

Each village keeps a copy of its own record and can correct it. Results are posted at the harbor within a week.

Trust depends on a record anyone can read.
