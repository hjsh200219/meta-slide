Three steps turn raw hourly readings into a trend that can be checked

1

Select the window

Keep only readings between 20:00 and 06:00 so every night is measured the same way.

2

Take the minimum

Record the lowest reading of each night, then average the nights of each year.

3

Fit the trend

Draw a least-squares line through the thirty-one annual values and report its slope.

Method note: no gaps were filled; years with fewer than 300 valid nights were excluded.
