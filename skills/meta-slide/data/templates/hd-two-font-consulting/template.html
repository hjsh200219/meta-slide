City Bus Network · 2026 Route Optimization Review

Merging overlapping routes cuts peak-hour waits by a quarter

A review of ridership and arrival data on 38 trunk routes shows that 11 overlap by more than 60% on the east–west corridor. At peak, buses arrive in bunches, followed by gaps of 20 minutes or more.

We recommend consolidating those 11 into 5 routes with no change in fleet size, and redeploying the saved headway to transfer hubs.

Approach

Three steps from data to a new network

1

Measure overlap

Match stop sequences across 38 routes and flag shared corridor segments above 60%.

2

Model headway

Replay GPS arrivals to see bunching, then simulate merged timetables at constant fleet size.

3

Reallocate service

Move the recovered trips to transfer hubs where waits are longest today.
